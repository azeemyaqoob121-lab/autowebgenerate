    Your local electrician and renewable energy specialist in the North East. 

    CC Electrical Services offers a comprehensive range of electrical services designed to cater to the unique needs of our clients. Whether you are a homeowner, business owner, or property manager, our skilled team is dedicated to delivering dependable and quality solutions.

    Our services are available in local areas including;

    Tyne & Wear, Newcastle, Sunderland, Gateshead, Durham, Northumberland, Middlesbrough, Hartlepool, Darlington

    Why choose us?

    Qualified and Accredited

    We are fully accredited with the necessary certification bodies for the work we do. MCS/RECC for Solar PV. OZEV for EV chargers, and NICEIC for all electrical scope. Giving you peace of mind that all our installations are performed to the highest standard, fully certified and insured.

    Quality Services

    We do not compromise on the quality of our services, ensuring that each project meets the highest standards of safety and efficiency. As your trusted electrician, we place a high emphasis on delivering nothing but the best.

    Customer Satisfaction

    We value the strong relationships we have built over the years with our customers, which are founded on trust, respect, and a shared commitment to excellence. Our team always goes above and beyond to ensure complete customer satisfaction.

    Competitive Prices

    We offer a wide range of electrical services at competitive prices without compromising quality or safety. Whether it's a small residential job or a light commercial management, we guarantee the best service within budget.
