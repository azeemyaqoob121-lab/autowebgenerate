About Us

Based out of our office in Bradford, C.C Electrical & Sons is a well-regarded electrical company that carries out all forms of domestic and commercial electrical work across Yorkshire. Formed in 1998, we have a proven track record of excellence in fuseboard replacements, house rewires, commercial new wiring, landlord certificates and other services in our core areas of Leeds and Wakefield.

We even offer nationwide coverage for commercial rewires, domestic rewires and other electrical works, although this applies to high-value jobs only. Call us on 01274 783598 (Bradford), 0113 871 5075 (Leeds) or 07834 319787 to determine your eligibility.

How We Can Help

Whether you require any fuseboard replacements, house rewires, commercial rewires or a landlord certificate, CC Electrical & Sons has your best interests at heart. With over 50 years of combined experience between us, our electricians are willing to turn their hand to any electrical fault you may be experiencing. We will assess all relevant circumstances and work hard to provide an optimal solution.

  • 24-hour Emergency Call Outs for Accidents, Repairs and Commercial Breakdowns
  • All Trades Provided In-house
  • One-year Workmanship Guarantee on All Services
  • Public Liability Insurance up to £10 Million

In addition to our guarantees, we will fix anything we have worked on if there is a fault after we have worked on it. All our electrical services are completed by electricians who hold NVQ Level 3 qualifications in Electrical Engineering, and as a company, we are a NIC domestic installer. CC Electrical & Sons is highly experienced, qualified and prepared to look after your property’s electrical systems.
